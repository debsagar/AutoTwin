Evidence and assumptions

Activity stations follow the BPMN model; lines collapse intervening routing gateways. Positions use BPMN drawing coordinates when available, otherwise a simple grid. They are illustrative process positions, not inferred warehouse geometry. Tokens are case events; orbiting means execution progress, not physical travel. Blue tokens indicate waiting; gold tokens indicate execution. Simultaneous activities may show several tokens. Synthetic external slots are assumed capacity, not real employees.
